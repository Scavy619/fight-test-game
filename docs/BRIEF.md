# Original brief (from the user, 2026-11-11)

Project: AI-futuristic mythological fighting game. A simple but visually impressive, futuristic, AI-inspired two-player fighting game combining martial arts, sword combat, ninja techniques, supernatural powers and mythology from cultures around the world.

## Core concept
- Two-player fighting game (Player 1 vs Player 2).
- Simple controls, easy to learn, with depth for experienced players.
- Futuristic graphics with an anime-inspired visual identity.
- Fast combat with smooth animations, combos, special attacks, blocks, dodges, finishing moves.
- Visually impressive arena with atmospheric effects.
- AI-assisted opponent for practice mode.

## Fighting styles to draw on
- Japanese: Jujutsu, Judo, Karate, Kendo, Kenjutsu and samurai sword fighting, Ninjutsu, shuriken throwing, anime-inspired energy attacks (spinning energy sphere).
- Indian: Kalaripayattu, Gatka, Silambam (staff), Malla-yuddha (wrestling), Indian sword and shield.
- Other: Chinese Kung Fu and Wushu, Muay Thai, Krav Maga, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Taekwondo, European medieval swordsmanship, Filipino Eskrima and Kali.

## Weapons and powers
Katana and dual swords; ninja swords; shuriken and throwing weapons; spears, staffs, tridents; bows and energy arrows; elements fire, water, lightning, wind, earth, ice; teleportation and shadow attacks; energy spheres; ultimate attacks and cinematic finishers. Each character gets a unique combination.

## Mythology
- Indian: Shiva, Vishnu, Kali, Durga, Indra, Hanuman, Kartikeya.
- Japanese: Amaterasu, Susanoo, Tsukuyomi, Raijin, Fujin, Hachiman.
- Greek: Zeus, Poseidon, Hades, Ares, Athena, Hercules.
- Norse: Odin, Thor, Loki, Freya, Tyr.
- Egyptian: Ra, Anubis, Horus, Osiris, Set.
- Chinese: Sun Wukong, Nezha, Erlang Shen.
- Celtic: Lugh, Morrigan, Cú Chulainn.
- Mesopotamian: Gilgamesh, Ishtar, Enki.
- Aztec: Quetzalcoatl, Huitzilopochtli.

Use mythology respectfully as inspiration for characters, arenas, powers, weapons and lore. Distinguish traditional mythology from original fictional interpretation.

## First version
Local two-player combat; single-player practice against AI; character selection; multiple fighting styles; specials and ultimates; health bars and energy meters; best-of-three rounds; multiple mythology-inspired arenas.

## Visual and technical direction
Futuristic anime-inspired graphics; neon and cinematic lighting; smooth animation; particle effects; detailed weapons and character designs; responsive controls; modular architecture for adding characters, weapons, gods, arenas.

## Phases
1. Research styles, weapons, mythology, mechanics.
2. Concept, storyline, roster, visual direction.
3. Playable prototype with two characters and basic combat.
4. Weapons, combos, specials, AI opponents.
5. Mythology characters, more arenas, new styles.
6. Polish graphics, animation, sound, balance.

Start simple, make combat enjoyable, build the universe progressively. Do not overcomplicate the first version. Playable beats ambitious but unfinished.
